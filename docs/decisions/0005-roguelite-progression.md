# ADR-0003

## Title

Permanent Rocket Upgrades

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The prototype has a complete gameplay loop but lacks long-term progression.

## Decision

Credits earned during missions may be spent on permanent rocket upgrades.

The first upgrade increases engine thrust.

## Consequences

Players can become stronger across multiple runs.

Future systems such as modular ship parts, crew progression, and homebase upgrades can build upon this foundation.
