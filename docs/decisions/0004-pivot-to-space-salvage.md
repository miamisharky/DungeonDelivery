# ADR-0002

## Title

Pivot to Space Salvage Roguelite

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The original concept was a fantasy delivery game.

During prototyping, the project direction evolved toward a more unique space salvage game with rocket movement, orbital cleanup, modular ship upgrades, roguelite progression, and eventual idle systems.

## Decision

The game will pivot to a 2D space salvage roguelite.

The player will pilot a rocket, collect space trash, deliver it to recyclers, earn credits, and upgrade their ship, crew, homebase, and cleaned planets over time.

## Consequences

Existing systems will be rethemed:

- Player becomes Rocket
- Package becomes Space Trash / Salvage
- Delivery Zone becomes Recycler
- Score becomes Credits
- Timer becomes Mission Time / Fuel Window

Future systems will support modular rocket construction, hazards, crew progression, and idle metaprogression.
