# Orbit Salvage Vision

## Elevator Pitch

Pilot a small modular rocket through cluttered orbital zones, collect space trash, deliver it to recycling stations, earn credits, and upgrade your ship, crew, and planetary cleanup network.

## Core Fantasy

I am a scrappy space salvager cleaning up dangerous orbits one mission at a time.

## Core Loop

Launch Mission

Pilot Rocket
↓
Collect Space Trash
↓
Avoid Hazards
↓
Deliver Salvage to Recycler
↓
Earn Credits
↓
Upgrade Rocket / Crew / Base
↓
Attempt Harder Missions
